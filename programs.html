Undergraduate Flagship

Bachelor of Science in Nursing (B.Sc. Nursing)

The 4-year B.Sc. Nursing degree program is designed to develop first-level nursing practitioners who possess broad medical knowledge, critical diagnostic judgment, manual dexterity, and ethical principles to care for individuals, families, and communities.

Quick Course Synopsis

Duration: 4 Academic Years (Full-Time)
Intake Capacity: 60 Seats per Batch
Affiliation: RGUHS, Bengaluru
Approval: INC New Delhi & KSNC
Eligibility: 10+2 / PUC Science (PCB 45%+)
Clinical Practice: 1000+ Bed Teaching Hospitals

Curricular Outline Across 4 Years:

  • Year 1: Applied Anatomy & Physiology, Applied Biochemistry & Nutrition, Nursing Foundations, Communicative English, Applied Psychology.
  • Year 2: Applied Microbiology, Pharmacology, Pathology & Genetics, Adult Health Nursing I (Medical Surgical Nursing) with Clinical Rotation.
  • Year 3: Child Health Nursing (Pediatrics), Mental Health Nursing (Psychiatry), Community Health Nursing I, Nursing Research & Statistics.
  • Year 4: Midwifery & Obstetrical Nursing, Community Health Nursing II, Nursing Management & Leadership, plus 6-Month Intensive Integrated Clinical Internship.
View Admission Guidelines

Career Opportunities

Graduates qualify for immediate appointments as Clinical Staff Nurses, Nursing Officers in AIIMS/Military Nursing, ICU Specialists, and are eligible to sit for NCLEX-RN (USA/Canada), UK NMC CBT/OSCE, and DHA/HAAD in the UAE.

Program Highlights

  • Upgrades GNM Diploma to a University Bachelor's Degree.
  • Enhances promotional ladder to Nursing Supervisor / Tutor.
  • Fulfills prerequisite criteria for foreign immigration point systems.
Professional Advancement

Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing (P.B.B.Sc)

A 2-year undergraduate bridge program designed specifically for in-service Registered Nurses who have completed a Diploma in General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM) and wish to expand their professional scope.

Duration: 2 Academic Years
Intake: 30 Seats
Eligibility: GNM Passed + RN/RM Registration
Affiliation: RGUHS, Bengaluru

Key Subjects Studied:

Advanced Medical Surgical Nursing, Maternal Nursing, Child Health Nursing, Nursing Education, Nursing Administration & Ward Management, Community Health, and Introduction to Nursing Research.

Postgraduate Specializations

Master of Science in Nursing (M.Sc. Nursing)

2-Year master's program empowering advanced nurse practitioners, clinical nurse specialists, researchers, and nursing educators.

Medical Surgical Nursing

Advanced sub-specialization tracks in Critical Care, Cardio-Thoracic Nursing, Oncology, Nephrology & Neurosciences with intensive ICU rotations.

OBG / Maternity Nursing

High-risk obstetrics, perinatal care, fetal monitoring, advanced labor suite delivery protocols, and gynecological surgical nursing care.

Child Health (Paediatric) Nursing

Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) management, pediatric emergencies, congenital anomalies care, and developmental milestone assessment.

Psychiatric (Mental Health) Nursing

Diagnostic psychopathology, clinical counseling, cognitive behavior therapy, addiction medicine, and psychopharmacology clinical postings.

Community Health Nursing

Public health epidemiology, primary healthcare management, maternal & child health programs, and rural health administration.

M.Sc. Eligibility

Registered Nurse and Midwife with B.Sc. Nursing or P.B.B.Sc. Nursing degree with minimum 55% aggregate marks and at least 1 year of clinical experience.

Core Healthcare Diploma

General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM)

A 3-year diploma course approved by the Karnataka State Nursing Council and Indian Nursing Council. GNM prepares bedside nurses with robust hands-on fundamentals, emergency first-aid proficiency, and patient care management.

Duration: 3 Years (including 6-month internship)
Eligibility: 10+2 Any Stream (min 40%) with English
Age Limit: 17 to 35 Years
Scope: Immediate employment in hospitals & clinics
