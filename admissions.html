Course Eligibility Criteria (2025-2026)

In strict compliance with Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences and Indian Nursing Council statutory requirements.

Course Duration Academic Requirement Minimum Age Seats
B.Sc. Nursing 4 Years Pass in 10+2 / PUC with Physics, Chemistry, Biology & English with min 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST). 17 Years as of 31st Dec 2025 60 Seats
Post Basic B.Sc. 2 Years Pass in GNM Diploma with valid registration as Registered Nurse & Midwife (RN/RM) with any State Nursing Council. No upper limit 30 Seats
M.Sc. Nursing 2 Years Pass in B.Sc. Nursing / P.B.B.Sc. with minimum 55% aggregate marks and at least 1 year of clinical experience. No upper limit 25 Seats
GNM Diploma 3 Years Pass in 10+2 / Higher Secondary in any stream (Science/Arts/Commerce) with minimum 40% aggregate and English. 17 - 35 Years 40 Seats

5-Step Admission Process

A seamless, student-centric admission process with complete counselor guidance.

1

Online Application

Submit student profile & select nursing course.

2

Verification of 10th, 12th / GNM marks cards.

3

Counseling & Interview

4

Provisional Allotment

Issuance of formal provisional admission letter.

5

Induction & Hostels

Orientation, uniform sizing & hostel allotment.

Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • SSLC / 10th Standard Marks Card (Proof of Date of Birth)
  • PUC / 10+2 / Higher Secondary Marks Card
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous institution
  • Migration Certificate (for students outside Karnataka)
  • Conduct & Character Certificate
  • 8 Recent Passport Size Photographs with white background
  • Registered Nurse Certificate (for P.B.B.Sc. & M.Sc. applicants)

Scholarship & Financial Aid

We believe deserving students should never be deprived of healthcare education due to financial hurdles.

BGI Academic Merit Scholarship:

Up to 25% fee waiver for candidates securing >85% aggregate in 10+2 PCB science.

Government Post-Matric Scholarships:

Full facilitation for SC/ST/OBC and Minority state scholarships through Karnataka SSP Portal.

Education Loan Assistance:

Authorized loan bonafide and fee estimation documents provided for all nationalized & private banks.
