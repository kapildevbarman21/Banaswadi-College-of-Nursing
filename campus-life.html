Campus Life & Infrastructure

A safe, supportive, and vibrant collegiate environment where academic focus converges with cultural richness and personal well-being.

High-Tech Learning

Specialized Nursing Laboratories

Fostering procedural accuracy, patient safety, and diagnostic confidence.

Nursing Foundation Lab

Over 10 hospital beds equipped with clinical adult and infant mannequins for mastering patient positioning, catheterization, vital signs, and bedside communication.

MCH & OBG Lab

Equipped with advanced gynecological simulators, delivery models, fetal monitors, and neonatal resuscitation units for hands-on maternity practice.

Anatomy & Physiology Lab

Contains full-sized human skeletons, articulated organ models, 3D anatomical charts, and micro-histology specimens for comprehensive pre-clinical training.

Nutrition & Dietetics Lab

Fully equipped kitchen stations for calculating therapeutic diet plans for diabetics, renal failure patients, cardiac disease, and pediatric nutritional deficits.

Community Health Lab

Furnished with rural family health kits, home delivery bags, immunization simulation cold chains, and visual health education audiovisual displays.

Computer & Audio-Visual Lab

High-speed internet workstations with statistical software (SPSS) for nursing research, e-learning modules, and hospital information system (HIS) practice.

Knowledge Repository

Digital Helinet Medical Library

Our library is a member of the Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences Helinet (Health Science Library & Information Network) Consortium, granting our faculty and students 24/7 digital access to over 5,000 international biomedical journals, e-books, Medline, and clinical pharmacology databases.

  • Over 6,500 printed nursing textbooks, monographs, and research theses.
  • Dedicated digital browsing cubicles with high-speed internet and printing.
  • National and international nursing periodicals (AJN, Lancet, Indian Journal of Nursing).

Separate Hostels for Boys & Girls

We offer dedicated, secure hostel facilities with round-the-clock security, resident wardens, and CCTV monitoring. Situated in a clean, quiet neighborhood in Byrathi, the hostels offer spacious, well-ventilated rooms with study tables, wardrobes, and power backup.

Student Nurses Association (SNA)

The Banaswadi College of Nursing SNA unit is an affiliated member of the Trained Nurses Association of India (TNAI). The association organizes annual sports meets, Lamp Lighting Ceremonies, Florence Nightingale Memorial lectures, World Health Day rallies, and blood donation drives.

Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy

Ragging in any form is strictly banned inside the college premises, laboratories, and hostels under Supreme Court and INC directives. Any student found engaging in harassment is liable for immediate expulsion and legal prosecution.
