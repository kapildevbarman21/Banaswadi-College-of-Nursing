Distinctive Hallmarks

Why Choose Banaswadi College of Nursing?

Rooted in the timeless motto "Knowledge is Divine," our institution delivers an academic ecosystem centered on ethical practice, clinical mastery, and global employability.

1000+ Bed Hospital Clinical Postings

Students gain immersive patient bedside exposure at premier government and multi-specialty super-specialty hospitals in Bengaluru across trauma, cardiology, NICU, and oncology.

Modern Simulation & OSCE Labs

Equipped with high-fidelity computerized mannequins, advanced birthing simulators, IV training arms, and surgical instrument setups mirroring modern hospital environments.

RGUHS Curriculum with Helinet e-Library

Direct access to the Rajiv Gandhi University Helinet consortium, offering thousands of international medical journals, clinical databases, e-books, and research repositories.

International Placements (NCLEX & OET)

Structured language and licensing support enables our graduates to step into distinguished nursing careers in the United Kingdom (NHS), USA, Canada, Germany, and the Middle East.

Safe, Serene Campus & Hostels

Situated in Byrathi Extension near Hennur Road, Bangalore, providing separate secure hostels for girls and boys, hygienic multi-cuisine dining, 24/7 security, and college transport.

Holistic Care & Community Outreach

Active Student Nurses Association (SNA), National Service Scheme (NSS), and regular community health camps serving rural villages in Bangalore North, fostering civic empathy.
