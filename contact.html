How to Reach Campus

From Kempegowda Int'l Airport (BLR):

Located just 22 km south of the airport via State Highway 104 / Bagalur-Hennur Link Road (approx. 25-30 minutes by taxi).

From Majestic (KSR Bengaluru Station):

Distance: 16 km. BMTC Direct Bus Routes: 293, 293A, 294, 295 from Majestic Kempegowda Bus Stand directly to Kothanur / Byrathi Gate.

Nearest Metro Station:

Baiyappanahalli Metro Station (Purple Line) and Nagawara / Hebbal are conveniently connected via frequent BMTC feeder feeder buses.
