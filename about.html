Institutional Overview & Legacy

Founded in 2005 under the visionary aegis of the Bangalore Group of Institutions (BGI), Banaswadi College of Nursing stands as a beacon of clinical excellence and medical ethics.

Two Decades of Excellence

Rooted in Healing, Driven by Knowledge

Banaswadi College of Nursing was established in 2005 in Bengaluru with the dedicated mission of addressing the global demand for highly competent, empathetic, and evidence-based nursing professionals. As a core constituent of the prestigious Bangalore Group of Institutions (BGI), the college is affiliated to the Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS), Bengaluru, recognized by the Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi, and approved by the Karnataka State Nursing Council (KSNC) and the Government of Karnataka.

Our serene campus located at Byrathi Extension off Hennur-Bagalur Main Road provides the ideal academic sanctuary. With clinical postings across 1000+ beds in premier government and private super-specialty teaching hospitals, our graduates stand at the forefront of healthcare worldwide.

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our Vision

To be globally recognized as a premier center of excellence in nursing education, clinical innovation, and evidence-informed patient care that fosters leadership and selfless humanitarian service.

To provide comprehensive education combining medical science with empathetic bedside care, instilling lifelong learning, critical inquiry, and global nursing competencies.

Core Values
