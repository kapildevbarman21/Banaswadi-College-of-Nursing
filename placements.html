Bridging Nursing Education to Rewarding Careers

The Career Guidance and Placement Cell at Banaswadi College of Nursing actively coordinates campus recruitment drives, pre-placement workshops, mock interviews, and licensing examinations. We maintain formal memoranda with prominent multi-specialty and super-specialty hospital chains.

Overseas Nursing Careers

International Licensing & Migration Cell

Comprehensive training for international healthcare registration.

United Kingdom (NHS)

Specialized coaching for the UK NMC Computer Based Test (CBT) and Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE). Band 5 Staff Nurse positions with visa sponsorship.

USA & Canada (NCLEX-RN)

Guidance for CGFNS credential verification and intensive question bank practice for the National Council Licensure Examination (NCLEX-RN).

Middle East (Gulf)

Preparation for Dubai Health Authority (DHA), Health Authority Abu Dhabi (HAAD/DoH), Saudi Prometric, and Qatar QCHP licensing examinations.

OET & IELTS Cell

Interactive language labs focusing on healthcare communication scenarios, clinical dialogue, nursing discharge summaries, and listening comprehension.
